Reading the project notebook

The notebook is the shared document. Every entry carries a status that says how far to trust it, and who wrote it.

Suggestion

Written by an agent

An idea. Nobody has stood behind it yet.

Fact

Written by an agent

An agent states it as true. Check the sources.

Verified

Only you can write it

You checked the evidence yourself.

Accepted

Only you can write it

Your decision. Agents must work to it.

Under the entries, Revision history and comparison lists every saved version with its editor. Compare revision shows two versions side by side.

The workspace page

Open the Project notebook view. At the top are the title, objective, constraints and open questions. Then the entries, under the heading Decisions, facts and suggestions. The status on each entry is the thing to read first. Suggestion and fact come from agents. Verified and accepted can only come from you, and no agent can change or remove them. So when you want to know what is actually settled, look for the two on the right. The history panel below shows who changed what; it is the quickest way to see what your agents did since you last looked.
