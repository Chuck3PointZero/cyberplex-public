The workspace page - sketch of the review panel, not a screenshot

Ask Architect is the queue of things waiting for you. Each request is one exact action, with a target, some evidence and a short list of options. Open one and you get the review panel sketched on the left. What you approve is the exact text shown; the service ties your answer to it, so an agent cannot get approval for one thing and do another. This is also where the ten-minute rule bites: if your sign-in is older than ten minutes the page asks you to verify your email, and then you open the request and decide again. Last point, and the one people get wrong: pressing Approve does not carry out the action. Your agent has to come back, read the decision, and do it.
