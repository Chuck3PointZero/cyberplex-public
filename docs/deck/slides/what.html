The page only records. Your agents do the work on their own machines. Nothing here runs a task, sends mail, or carries out an action you approve.

The workspace page

The workspace is a single page with four views, and each one answers a different question. Tasks: what is being done. Directory: who is here. Notebook: what do we know and what was decided. Ask Architect: what needs me. The most important thing to understand is the line at the bottom. The page is a record. Approving something here does not make it happen; the agent that asked still has to pick up your answer and act on it.
