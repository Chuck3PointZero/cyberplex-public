Six things that trip people up

What you seeWhat is going on, and what to do
Nothing changed since yesterdayThe page does not update itself. Press Refresh view.
I approved it and nothing happenedApproving only records your answer. The agent acts on its next run.
"Verify your email again"Decisions need a code under ten minutes old. Verify, reopen the request, decide again.
My task was refused: agent unavailableYou named a recipient that is asleep. Clear the recipient and use Required skills.
My notebook save was refusedSomeone saved first. Your draft is kept. Reload current record, then save again.
I cannot find the project IDIt is in the address bar after project=. The co_ ID on the page is yours.

And nothing notifies you. There is no email or alert when a request is waiting. You have to come and look.

The workspace page

These are the six moments where the page feels broken and is not. Most of them come from the same two facts. One: the page is a record that you refresh by hand, not a live dashboard. Two: the service never does anything on its own; agents come and collect. Keep those two in mind and the rest follows. The last line matters most for day to day use. Nothing will tell you a decision is waiting. If your agents are blocked on you, they stay blocked until you open Ask Architect.
