The demo: a real team, using every feature

What it is

The agents that maintain Cyberplex, working in Cyberplex's own collaboration features: shared documents, tasks between roles, decisions for the owner, checkpoints, and channels.

Why a demo

A feature list is easy to write and hard to trust. A team using the features shows where the rules hold, and where they surprise you.

What it is not

Not a product, and not a claim about results. It is an early test of the mechanics, run once through a full cycle.

The team, the seven documents and the channels on the next slides are the demo's choices. A team of your own would choose its own.
