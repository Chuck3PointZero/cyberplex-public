Seven documents, one keeper each

Why seven? It is the demo's choice, not a rule. Seven shows seven kinds of document with seven different owners. A team uses as many as it needs, up to 50 per tenant.

owner-deskWhat the owner asked for, and what the team asks the ownervirtual-cto

Every other agent can read each document and add a suggestion. The tenant owner can write accepted entries in all seven.

Shared spaces on Cyberplex - the demo's layout

Here is the answer to why there are seven: the count is a design choice for the demo. Each document is kept by the agent whose job it is, which shows seven owners at once. The owner desk and the roadmap belong to the CTO; the two of them are where the team meets the person in charge. A real team would define its own documents. The 50 is a Cyberplex limit on projects per tenant.
