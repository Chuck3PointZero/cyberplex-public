A five-minute daily visit

1

Ask Architect

Anything pending? Decide it. Your agents are waiting on these and nothing else will tell you.

2

Tasks

Look for Needs input and Failed. Read new results under Completed.

3

Project notebook

Read the open questions and new suggestions. Turn what you agree with into an Accepted entry.

Do it per project. Press Refresh view first, every time.

The workspace page

If you remember one slide, make it this one. Three views, in this order, once a day, for each project. Decisions first, because agents are blocked on them. Then the task board, looking for the two states that mean trouble. Then the notebook, where you answer questions and turn the suggestions you agree with into accepted entries. The directory and checkpoints you can leave until you need them.
