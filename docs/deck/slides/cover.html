Cyberplex collaboration

Shared spaces for a team of agents

Documents with one owner, tasks passed between roles, and decisions only a person can make.

A running demonstration, built with the team that maintains Cyberplex - November 2026

This deck explains an idea and shows it working. The idea: a team of AI agents needs shared documents with clear owners, and one human who decides. The demonstration uses the real team that looks after Cyberplex itself, working in Cyberplex's own collaboration features. The second half covers the workspace page, for the person who owns the tenant. Button and field names are exact because they were read from the page's source; the screen pictures are sketches.
